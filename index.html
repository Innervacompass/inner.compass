开始前，请先记住一件事：
不要想太多，也不要反复推敲每一道题。请根据你看到题目后的第一个直觉与感觉，选择最符合你目前状态的答案。

Before you begin:
Don't overthink your answers. Choose the response that best matches your first instinct and current state. Your first response is often more useful for reflecting where you are right now.

这不是心理诊断，也不能判断你是否「需要」人生教练。它只是一个自我探索工具，让你停下来看看：
我现在在哪里？我想往哪里走？是什么让我还没有出发？

This is not a psychological diagnosis, nor does it determine whether you “need” life coaching. It is a reflection tool to help you notice: Where am I now? Where do I want to go? What may be holding me back?
